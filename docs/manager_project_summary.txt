Lead Validation and Follow-Up Automation

This project is a real estate lead management system that helps sales teams process uploaded leads, validate contact details, score lead quality, generate outreach drafts, and manage follow-up activity from one dashboard.

Core Workflow

1. The user uploads a fixed-format CSV or Excel file containing lead details.
2. The system validates required fields, email format/status, and phone status.
3. Duplicate leads are detected using email and phone number.
4. Valid leads are scored based on source, budget, timeline, location, requirement, and contact validation.
5. Leads are categorized as Hot, Warm, or Cold.
6. Qualified leads can be assigned to agents based on territory and property type.
7. The system generates AI-assisted email drafts using lead details, old templates, and approved learning examples.
8. The sales user can review, edit, approve, reject, save, and send the email draft.
9. Follow-up status, conversation history, learning feedback, and call history are tracked.

Main Features

- CSV and Excel lead upload
- Required field validation
- Email and phone validation
- Duplicate lead detection and duplicate resolution
- Lead scoring and Hot/Warm/Cold categorization
- Agent assignment support
- AI email draft generation using existing templates and learning history
- Human review for approving or rejecting AI-generated drafts
- AI learning admin page for managing gold examples, error examples, and generation logs
- Manual Twilio voice call support for validated and qualified leads
- Call eligibility checks, call cooldown, Twilio text-to-speech script, and call history
- Dashboard for lead pipeline, scoring, and follow-up visibility

Safety and Control

- Invalid, duplicate, cold, unqualified, opted-out, and do-not-call leads are blocked from calling.
- Calls are manual only and cannot be triggered in bulk.
- Lead phone numbers are never editable in the call popup.
- Twilio and AI API keys stay on the backend and are not exposed in frontend code.
- AI-generated emails are reviewable and editable before sending.
- Admin can remove learning examples from future AI use without deleting the log.

Business Value

The system reduces manual lead checking, helps sales teams prioritize high-quality leads, creates consistent outreach, and keeps a clear audit trail for validation, scoring, email drafts, AI learning, and manual sales calls.
